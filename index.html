How these numbers are computed

  • Medians, not averages. A few extreme sales would pull an average far from a typical home, so every figure is a median.
  • Price rule. Price figures use only sales from $10,000 to $5,000,000. Sales outside that range are implausible for a home in these towns.
  • Days on market is the contract date minus the list date: the time from listing to an accepted offer. Sales missing either date are skipped, never counted as zero. Many skipped sales never had a public listing (off-market or builder sales), so this figure describes homes that were listed.
  • Miles to campus is the straight-line distance to the nearest campus's landmark, not driving distance. It uses every sale.
  • Every sale counts. Each sale is identified by its listing ID. Sales that share a street address (such as units in one condo building) are separate sales and are all kept.
  • Not used: square feet of living area, lot size and the reported home type. Living area and lot size are not needed for any figure here, and home type is unreliable in this dataset.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026. Values are as reported by the collection source, not verified against county records.
